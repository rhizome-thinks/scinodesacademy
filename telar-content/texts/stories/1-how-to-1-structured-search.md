---
title: "1. Structured Search"
---

The traditional way to earch for academic literature online. 

Use keywords and Boolean operators such as AND, OR and NOT to search titles and abstracts of publications. 

For example:

"cancer lung cancer AND mitochondria"
"battery AND sodium ion NOT lithium"
"remote work OR telework OR online work"
