---
title: "2 . Semantic Search"
---

A newer way to search for academic literature online using AI models.

Describe what you’re looking for in everyday language or frame it as a question. Semantic search finds publications based on the meaning of your query. 

For example:

“Research on batteries that use sodium ions in place of lithium ions”
“How do mitochondria affect the growth of lung cancer cells?”
“How does working from home affect collaboration among colleagues?”
